## What is it good for?

A calm, low-detail image that sits behind icons and text without competing with them.

## How do I adjust it?

- Change the number of ridges to control depth: three feels open, five feels layered.
- Name the time of day instead of listing colours; the sky gradient follows it.
- The marked phrases in the prompt show what each part controls.
