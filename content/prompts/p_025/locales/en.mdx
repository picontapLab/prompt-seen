## What is it good for?

Keeps the product shape readable and leaves a clean area for a label or logo.

## How do I adjust it?

- Name the tile shapes (quarter circles, triangles, half circles) to keep the pattern varied.
- Say “seamless” if the pattern will repeat across a larger surface.
