## What is it good for?

Keeps the product shape readable and leaves a clean area for a label or logo.

## How do I adjust it?

- Describe the object's silhouette first, then the label; the label stays in proportion.
- A soft circular backdrop separates a pale product from a pale wall.
- To change the subject or colours, start from the [product poster template](template:t_001).

Compare it with tins and bottles in the [packaging examples](use-case:packaging).
