## What is it good for?

A calm, low-detail image that sits behind icons and text without competing with them.

## How do I adjust it?

- Describe brush texture (dry brush, wet wash) rather than line thickness.
- Place the seal explicitly; otherwise it may land on the subject.
- To change the subject or colours, start from the [ink scroll template](template:t_002).
