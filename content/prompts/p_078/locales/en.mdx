## What is it good for?

Reads at a glance in a feed: one clear subject, strong contrast and room for a caption.

## How do I adjust it?

- Ask for a top-down view to keep the bowl round and the layout tidy.
- List toppings with positions; unplaced toppings tend to pile up in the centre.
