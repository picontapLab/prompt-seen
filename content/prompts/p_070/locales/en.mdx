## What is it good for?

Works when cropped to a circle: the subject sits in the centre with a simple background.

## How do I adjust it?

- Count the leaves; fewer, larger leaves read better at small sizes.
- Mention a pot or a cropped edge to decide whether the plant floats or stands.
