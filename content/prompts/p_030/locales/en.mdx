## What is it good for?

Flat shapes and a limited palette print cleanly at large sizes and look deliberate on a wall.

## How do I adjust it?

- Change the number of ridges to control depth: three feels open, five feels layered.
- Name the time of day instead of listing colours; the sky gradient follows it.
