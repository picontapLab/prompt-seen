## How to use

Use the exact recorded prompt and the displayed original reference assets in order. Changes to the prompt or inputs create an untested variant.

## Observed result

Pencil owl blinks and lifts its wings while remaining a drawing on the notebook page. Six-frame composed GIF preserves the paper setting with small page and line drift between frames.

## GIF assembly

The model generated a 3-column by 2-row PNG sprite sheet. Crop equal cells in row-major order and encode six frames at 300 ms per frame with infinite looping. The resulting GIF is 512 × 512 pixels and 1.8 seconds per cycle. No interpolation was added. This is a composed animation, not a native GIF returned by the model.
