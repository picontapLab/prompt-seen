## How to use

Upload the first adult portrait, then the second adult portrait. Image 1 stays on the left; image 2 stays on the right. The demonstration uses AI-generated fictional people. Keep all lettering in English.

## What to check

Inspect identity, layout and every requested caption at full size and at phone display size.
