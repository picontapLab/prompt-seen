## GIF assembly

The model generated a 3-column by 2-row PNG sprite sheet. Crop equal cells in row-major order and encode six frames at 300 ms per frame with infinite looping. The resulting GIF is 512 × 512 pixels and 1.8 seconds per cycle. No interpolation was added. This is a composed animation, not a native GIF returned by the model.
