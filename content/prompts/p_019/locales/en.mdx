## What is it good for?

Works when cropped to a circle: the subject sits in the centre with a simple background.

## How do I adjust it?

- Name two rim colours, one for each side, to keep the outline readable.
- Keep the face in shadow; detail inside the silhouette breaks the effect.
- To change the subject or colours, start from the [neon portrait template](template:t_003).
