## What is it good for?

A calm, low-detail image that sits behind icons and text without competing with them.

## How do I adjust it?

- Move the horizon with words like “low horizon” or “high horizon” to change the mood.
- Ask for horizontal reflection lines to keep the water flat and graphic.
