## How to use

Upload one pet photo and choose an adult age range and personality. The example pet is synthetic.

Resemblance should come from attitude and colors, with fully human anatomy.

## Recorded result

The example was generated with the built-in image tool. Its exact model ID was not returned. The recorded run contains the submitted prompt and ordered inputs.
