## What is it good for?

Bold geometry that stays legible from across a room, with bars reserved for event details.

## How do I adjust it?

- Give every shape a position (upper right, lower left); the layout stays balanced.
- Keep the palette to four colours plus the background.
- The marked phrases in the prompt show what each part controls.
