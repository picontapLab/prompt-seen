## What is it good for?

Shop banners, launch posts and packaging mock-ups where the product shape has to read instantly.

## How do I fill it in?

- **Product**: name the object and its material, such as “a speckled ceramic mug”. The material decides the surface texture.
- **Selling points**: up to three short phrases. Concrete ones (“airtight lid”) turn into visible details; vague ones (“premium”) are ignored.
- **Headline**: optional. The template reserves bars for it instead of asking for lettering, which often comes out garbled.

## What changed in version 2?

Version 2 adds the optional headline, a grey and a coral palette, and more ratios. Examples made with version 1 keep their original prompt.
