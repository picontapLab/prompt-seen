## What is it good for?

Banners, wide scroll wallpapers and covers that need a quiet, East Asian feel. In wide ratios the empty space matters more than the subject.

## How do I fill it in?

- **Subject**: one main subject, such as “a bamboo grove” or “a lone pine”. More subjects leave less empty space.
- **Brushwork**: decides the texture. Wet washes are soft; dry brush is rough.
- **Details**: up to four, and countable, such as “five tall stalks”.

The prompt itself stays in English: in testing, brushwork described in English came out more consistently.
