## Reaction stickers

Six nine-panel reaction sheets cover everyday replies, dog moods, office chats, social energy, encouragement and gaming. The portrait and dog sets need one reference photo each; the cat, raccoon, frog and ghost sets work from text alone. Each sheet is a single image to share. Exporting individual stickers is a separate workflow.

## Photo edits and shareable images

Make a two-person photo booth, a fictional pet newspaper, a two-panel meme, a weekend collage, a birthday card or a four-panel dog comic. The collage needs no reference photo; the other five need one or two as described in their input instructions. The three demonstration references are AI-generated. All image captions and generation prompts are in English, including on translated pages. Edit the nine captions in the six sticker templates or the name and age in the birthday template.
