Choose a studio hero image when lighting and composition set the mood. For a store listing, compare a clean white packshot. With a usable product photo, try a new angle and compare shape details against the reference.

For multiple scenes built from the same bottle photo, see the [nine-panel product campaign](prompt:p_123). The [product image guide](category:product) also compares new camera angles with targeted edits.
