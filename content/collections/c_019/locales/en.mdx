## Choose photos for your product

- Jewelry and glass benefit from a reflection study, joint close-up and scale reference.
- Clothing and bags need a flat view, stitching, the chosen color and a styling preview. Add lettering for custom work.
- Electronics and tools need the back, ports, dimensions and accessories. Use steps for moving parts.
- Furniture and storage need the back, footprint, measured dimensions and a room scene. Allow space for moving parts.
- Food and gift sets need the outer package, count and unboxed contents. Keep mandatory labels legible in a real photo.
- For a unique used item, start with its original overall photo and add a separate close-up for every defect.

Amazon US specifies a white main image and category-specific framing. On Google Merchant Center, retain the required IPTC digital source metadata in the final file. Etsy sellers should check the actual-photo rules for their category. For used items on eBay, use original photographs of the specific item and its defects. Check current channel rules before uploading.
