## What goes into an ink prompt?

Describe brush texture rather than line weight: “dry brush” and “wet washes” are more reliable than “thick lines” or “thin lines”. Then place the seal, or it may land on the subject.

Start with the [plum branch in ink](prompt:p_012), or go straight to the [ink scroll template](template:t_002).
