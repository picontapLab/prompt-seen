Start with where the image will appear: a reply, a feed post or a small animated attachment. Check how text, crops and timing hold up at phone size.

- [Portrait reaction stickers](prompt:p_274) uses one portrait to create nine English chat reactions. Check each caption, and crop or export the individual stickers separately.
- The text-only [sage tile banner](prompt:p_099) is a still graphic. Keep added text clear of the pattern and check the crop on the target platform.
- The [Monday reaction GIF](prompt:p_287) uses one character reference. Its nine frames loop in 2.4 seconds, with slight jitter around the Chinese lettering.

Compare [reaction stickers](collection:c_015) and [nine-frame social GIFs](collection:c_016) before choosing a format.
