Choose an example with a similar packaging shape and label placement. Review generated lettering at full size, then set production copy and confirm dimensions, dielines and bleed with your printer.

- The [green tea tin poster](prompt:p_024) uses an English text prompt to show a cylindrical package. Follow the wrap of the label and verify small type.
- The [honey jar label](prompt:p_043) uses text to show a front-facing label on a jar. Check how label spacing looks against the curved glass.
- The [skincare tube on blue](prompt:p_053) uses zero reference images. Use its front-facing layout to compare the proportions of a tube and wordmark; verify the lettering before sharing.
