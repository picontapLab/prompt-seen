Match the prompt to what you have and what you need to preserve. An avatar can begin with two lighting colors. A photo edit starts from visual details you can compare with the original.

- The text-only [amber and teal avatar](prompt:p_019) uses two rim lights to pick out a silhouette. It is suitable for a circular crop and does not represent a specific person.
- [Everyday reaction stickers](prompt:p_274) uses one synthetic portrait for a nine-panel sheet. Review identity cues and captions in every panel.
- The [old-photo restoration](prompt:p_138) uses a synthetic aged photo to show reduced scratches and fading. Generated facial or background details cannot establish historical facts.

Try the [neon portrait template](template:t_003) to change the subject or lighting, or compare photo-driven sheets in the [reaction stickers collection](collection:c_015).
