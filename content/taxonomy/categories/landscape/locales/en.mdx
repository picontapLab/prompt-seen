## How are these landscapes built?

Almost every landscape here is a stack of shapes ordered from light to dark, with one light source. The prompts name the number of layers and the time of day rather than individual colours.

## Which one should I start with?

- For a wallpaper: [Morning mist over the hills](prompt:p_016), which leaves the sky empty.
- For a print: [Desert dunes in warm light](prompt:p_006).
- For an ink painting: the [ink scroll template](template:t_002).
