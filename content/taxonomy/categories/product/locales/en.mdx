Choose the prompt by what needs to stay consistent: the product across several scenes, its shape from a new angle, or the rest of the photo during an edit.

- [Campaign grid from one photo](prompt:p_123) uses one synthetic bottle reference for nine scenes. Check the label and cap in each product view, especially where the composition crops the bottle.
- [New angle for ceramic packaging](prompt:p_153) uses one bottle reference to reveal the lid and side handle. The output infers geometry hidden in the source view.
- [Vase recolor with markup](prompt:p_218) uses the photo first and a separate marked-up image second. Compare the finish as well as the color, since the blue glaze looks glossier.

Compare more input choices in the [commercial visuals collection](collection:c_009), or adapt the colors and scenes with the [campaign grid template](template:t_017).
