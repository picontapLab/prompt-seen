Decide where the final lettering will come from. Reserve space for precise typesetting, or ask for words in the output and compare every character and number against the brief.

- The text-only [jazz club poster](prompt:p_045) combines geometric shapes with generated title lettering. It shows how a headline can be integrated; add a verified event date yourself.
- The text-only [blue Bauhaus square](prompt:p_097) organizes circles and dots above two horizontal bands. The blank light band can carry typeset copy after you check the crop.
- The text-only [market opportunity slide](prompt:p_217) includes chart labels and figures. Its data are fictional, and all values and added text need checking before reuse.

See [posters with a clear hierarchy](collection:c_001) for reserved headline space or use the [product poster template](template:t_001) to try a short title.
