Create a richly textured palette-knife oil painting of a narrow snowy village lane. A turquoise stucco house with ochre door and glowing lantern stands at left; lilac flowering branches spill across its facade despite the fresh white snow. An orange-leaved tree arches above the lane and pale pastel houses lead toward a delicate church tower in the distance. Use broken impasto strokes, lavender shadows, creamy snow, flashes of gold and turquoise, and a dreamlike coexistence of seasons. Keep a strong perspective path from the lower-right foreground into the town. Vertical 4:5 artwork with visible paint ridges and no text.
