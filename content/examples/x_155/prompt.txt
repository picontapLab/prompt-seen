An empty underground staff dining area that feels strangely familiar: faded booth seating, tiled floors, fluorescent lights, metal railings and a corridor that disappears into darkness. Arrange the space plausibly but with one subtly awkward architectural transition. Use a level handheld camera, muted colors, low contrast, imperfect fluorescent color and the texture of an old compact-camera photograph. No people, monsters, blood, warning signs or dramatic fog; unease should come from the ordinary place itself.
