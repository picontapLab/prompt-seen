Paint a peaceful alpine lake from the stone terrace of a rustic lakeside cottage. A black cat sits with its back to us on the wall, watching distant snow-covered mountains and a village across blue reflective water. Place a wooden chair with a red plaid blanket and a small table with a cup and closed books on the left. Frame the view with red autumn leaves overhead and pink-purple flowers in pots along the terrace. Use thick expressive oil-paint marks, luminous cool blues, warm late-afternoon highlights and soft pastel clouds. Make the scene detailed yet painterly, with the cat as the quiet focal point. Landscape composition, no lettering.
