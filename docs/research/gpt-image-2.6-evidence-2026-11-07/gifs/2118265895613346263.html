Comedy contrast: the fox's giant square face and deadpan unblinking eyes remain 100% motionless across all frames, while [填动作，如: its two front paws slowly clap together]. Floating cleanly in the air ABOVE the head (with a wide empty transparent gap between the sign and the ears, NO sticks, NO poles), a flat rectangular white label with a crisp black border and bold black Chinese text reads '[填配字，如: 好棒棒]'. Isolated on a pure transparent background. Strictly NO white sticker border, NO white outline. Smooth 9-frame looping animation." 2️⃣ 微信 240×240 GIF89a 深色模式零白沫量化核心代码（Pillow）： def save_wechat_gif(frames_rgba, out_path, duration_ms=110): p_frames = [] for f in frames_rgba: rgba = f.convert("RGBA") alpha = rgba.getchannel("A") # 关键：千万别往白底上预乘！只保留 alpha>=145 的前景原始 RGB 参与调色板量化 clean_rgb = Image.new("RGB", rgba.size, (128, 128, 128)) fg_mask = alpha.point(lambda a: 255 if a >= 145 else 0) clean_rgb.paste(rgba.convert("RGB"), mask=fg_mask) quant = clean_rgb.quantize(colors=255, method=Image.Quantize.FASTOCTREE, dither=Image.Dither.NONE) # 255 号色固定留给透明通道，深色模式下边缘绝对不泛白沫 trans_mask = alpha.point(lambda a: 255 if a < 145 else 0) quant.paste(255, mask=trans_mask) p_frames.append(quant) p_frames[0].save(out_path, save_all=True, append_images=p_frames[1:], loop=0, duration=duration_ms, disposal=2, transparency=255, optimize=True)
