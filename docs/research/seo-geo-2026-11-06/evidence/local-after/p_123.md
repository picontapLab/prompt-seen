## Prompt · English

```text
Using the uploaded product photo, design a 3-by-3 campaign contact sheet. Keep the container, label and brand palette consistent across all nine panels. Use this campaign palette: sage green, ivory and brushed silver. In reading order the nine panels show: hero shot; cap and label close-up; hand holding the bottle; brushed metal detail; three-bottle repeated arrangement; floating bottle; paint texture macro; bottle beside leaves; bottle on a picnic table. Use coherent lighting, even gutters and a 4:5 canvas.
```

Customize: [3x3 product campaign prompt template](https://promptseen.dev/en/templates/product-brand-story-grid/)

## What each phrase does

- **A** `cap and label close-up`: The second panel reserves space for the cap texture and readable PINE mark.
- **B** `three-bottle repeated arrangement`: Repeating the same product establishes a coherent campaign rhythm.

## How to use

Upload one product photo; supply a palette and exactly nine scene themes.

Keep the product silhouette, cap and label consistent in every panel.

## Recorded result

The two test prints use the same synthetic PINE bottle reference, not a customer product photo. The default run uses sage green, ivory and brushed silver; the alternate run changes the palette and nine scene descriptions. Each print below pairs its input with its own output and submitted prompt.

Both outputs contain nine panels. The bottle, silver cap and PINE lettering remain recognizable in the product views; the material and paint close-ups do not show a whole bottle. These are observations of these two outputs, not a guarantee for other packaging or small label text.
