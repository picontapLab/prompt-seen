## Prompt · English

```text
Use the attached sculptural ceramic perfume bottle as a shape and material reference. Produce a studio packshot on seamless white from a distinct elevated three-quarter angle: clearly show the lid top, front body and hollow handle on the side. Keep the cream speckled ceramic, irregular stone-like cap, gold collar, handle opening and original proportions intact. Neutral white balance, crisp edges, generous breathing room, square image. No text or other objects.
```

## Generation evidence and limits

This example uses one synthetic ceramic perfume-bottle reference. It asks for a new camera angle on seamless white, not a new package design. Compare the cap, collar, handle opening and body proportions with the reference shown in the test print. The output is a generated interpretation; this single example does not establish exact geometry from unseen sides.
